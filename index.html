01 / The motivation

The person on
the ground.

Seeing individual soldiers pursued by FPV drones motivated Jan to explore a way for the person on the ground to defend themselves.

Jan Pilsinger / Founder & Austrian reserve officer

Following the war in Ukraine since its beginning brought that question into sharp focus for Jan. His military experience made it personal; his engineering background gave him a way to start exploring an answer.

BirdEater focuses on last-resort self-protection: an individual faces an immediate drone threat and has little time to respond. That is the situation motivating our work.

02 / The concept

An existing platform.
A new protective concept.

BirdEater is a 40 mm net-deployment concept intended for compatibility with existing stand-alone and underbarrel grenade launchers.

The development goal is an additional protective option that fits equipment soldiers already carry. Compatibility, interception performance and cost remain to be validated.

Designed for self-protection

03 / The first breakthrough

From simulation
to physical deployment.

Our first prototype launch test successfully deployed the net. For us, this was the moment the project moved beyond simulation.

  1. 01 / Modelled

    Simulation

    We developed and simulated the concept to inform our initial prototype work.

  2. 02 / Built

    Rapid prototyping

    We produced 3D-printed parts and built a physical prototype as a two-person team.

  3. 03 / Observed

    First deployment

    In an initial launch test, the prototype successfully deployed its net.

What this milestone establishes

A first demonstration of physical net deployment, reported by the team. It does not yet establish drone interception performance, deployment timing, operational range or compatibility with service launchers.
