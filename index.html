02 / The invention

The winding we filed.

  1. 01

    Wound

    Kevlar on a steel spool, packed so the spin after the muzzle pays it out.

  2. 02

    Spread

    Steel spheres carry the net. Design target: 80 cm across.

  3. 03

    Time

    Open within 10 m and within 200 ms. Both figures are from simulation.

03 / The net

A net, not a warhead.

Opened, the round is a hub and a net. Kevlar was chosen on calculation, to hold heat and a cutting edge, so a rotor fouls in the line instead of parting it. That choice is not yet a firing trial.

The engagement, as designed, is 30 to 40 metres. About as far as the aircraft is seen in time. The form is the 40mm a grenade launcher already fires, under the barrel or on its own.

04 / Use

Employment concepts.

Not trials. A spotter in a vehicle, and an officer over a crowd. In both, the launcher is already on the weapon in their hands. Underbarrel, or stand-alone 40mm. No second gun.

05 / The test

One shot on our stand.

We are asking European defence hackathons for a place to fire one complete opening shot. The stand is ours. It is not a service grenade launcher, and the round is not a brass cartridge. Nothing on this page has been fired yet.
